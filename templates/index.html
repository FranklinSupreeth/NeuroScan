How it works

From pixels to prediction

  1. 01

    Preprocess

    The scan is resized to 128×128 and converted to grayscale so every image is compared on equal terms.

  2. 02

    HOG

    Histogram of Oriented Gradients captures edges and shape — the outline of masses and anatomy.

  3. 03

    LBP + GLCM

    Local Binary Patterns and Gray-Level Co-occurrence statistics describe tissue texture: contrast, homogeneity, energy.

  4. 04

    SVM

    Features are standardised and passed to a Support Vector Machine that outputs a probability for each class.
